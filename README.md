# Construction of Colimits in the Category of Locally Ringed Spaces
 Bachelor thesis in Mathematics, Ulm University.

 **Grade:** 1.0

 This thesis gives an explicit construction of colimits in the category of locally ringed spaces (LRS). After developing the necessary categorical and sheaf-theoretic foundations, coproducts and coequalisers in LRS are constructed explicitly and used to obtain general colimits.

 ## Topics

 - Category theory and universal properties
 - Yoneda lemma
 - Limits and colimits
 - Directed colimits and finite limits
 - Sheaves and locally ringed spaces
 - Coproducts in LRS
 - Products in LRS
 - Construction of colimits
